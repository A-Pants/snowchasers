Did you know?

Ski down the mountain and find four torn pieces of an old map. Each one is hiding somewhere different: a weather station, a cliff kicker, a cabin in the trees, and something frozen.

Left stick leans. Push forward to go (it works on flat ground too), pull back to brake, push sideways to carve.

Right stick tricks. Pull down to load, flick up to hop. After a hop, push the left stick to flip (up and down) or spin (sideways). Off a ramp, let go of the stick first. The right stick grabs.

On a computer it is all keyboard: WASD for the left stick and the arrow keys for the right. No mouse or trackpad needed. Gamepads and touchscreens work too.

Press E (or the Walk mode button) to take your skis off and walk, which makes the fragments easy to pick up. Land a half-turn and you keep riding backwards (switch) until you turn back around.

Land tricks for style points and ski all the way down to the base to unlock new gear.
